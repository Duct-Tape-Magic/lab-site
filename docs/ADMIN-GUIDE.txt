OHS CHEM LABS - ADMIN GUIDE
===========================

The site: https://ohschemlabs.com
Students open labs, fill in answers, tables and photos, and download a PDF
report to submit in Canvas. Admins build the labs and run the site from the
site itself. Nothing needs installing.


1. LOGGING IN
-------------
Click "Log in" (top right). Admins sign in with Google, or with email and
password under "Admin sign in". After you log in, a dark admin bar appears at
the top of every page. You are signed out after 24 hours without activity.

Admin bar buttons, left to right:
  + Lab            create a lab
  + Folder         create a folder (a course, a unit, anything)
  Site text        edit every piece of wording students see
  Design           colours, card layout, page widths and spacing
  Stats            activity, students, and their saved entries
  Help             questions students sent from the "Get help" button
  Classes          access codes for students (see section 6)
  Admins           add or remove other admins
  View as student  see the site exactly as a student does
  Log out


2. THE HOME PAGE
----------------
Folders and labs appear together in one grid, in the order you choose. In
admin mode every card has Edit and Delete buttons plus left/right arrows that
move it earlier or later. A lab can sit before a folder; the order is yours.

Hidden or admin-only folders and draft labs show a badge and are invisible to
students. "View as student" is the quickest way to check what they see.


3. CREATING AND EDITING A LAB
-----------------------------
Click "+ Lab", or "Edit lab" on an existing one. The form, top to bottom:

  Title and link name   The link name becomes the address (#/lab/your-name).
                        Once a lab is published, leave the link name alone so
                        students' bookmarks keep working.
  Folder, Summary       Summary shows on the card and in search only.
  Search tags           Never shown to students; help the search box.
  Estimated minutes     Procedure time, shown on the card and lab page.
  Report write-up       Optional second estimate for the write-up.
  Order on the page     Lower numbers come first.
  Status                Draft (admins only), Published, or Scheduled.
  Who can open it       Everyone, password, selected classes/accounts, or
                        admins only. "Same as its folder" is the default.
  Get help button       Tick to show a "Get help" button at the top of the
                        lab. Then tick which admins get an email when a
                        student asks, and optionally write the automatic reply
                        the student sees the moment they send.
  Cover image           A picture for the card. Transparent PNGs sit on the
                        card colour with no box. Use the Zoom slider to make
                        icons and screenshots a similar size.
  Photos and videos     A slideshow shown under the lab.
  Procedure format      Builder (the normal choice), Lab file (the plain-text
                        form of the same lab), or Rich text (a simple editor
                        for pages with no interactive parts).
  Files                 PDFs, Word, Excel and so on, offered as downloads.

Click Save at the bottom. A copy of the previous version is kept
automatically every time you save.

Safety: if students have already saved work in a lab, the site warns you
before any change that would hide it, such as deleting a question, renaming
its permanent name, or changing a table's columns. Rewording, reordering and
adding blocks are always safe. Deleting a lab or a seat that has student work
asks you to type DELETE.


4. THE BUILDER
--------------
The Builder shows the lab as a list of blocks. Students see them in order.
Each block is a card: click the small triangle to open it, use the arrows to
move it, the copy icon to duplicate, the eye to jump to it in the preview,
the plus to insert a block after it, and the cross to delete it. Drag the
handle on the left to reorder.

Procedure blocks (the buttons under the Procedure list):
  Heading         A title for a part of the lab.
  Text            Writing. Markdown works (## headings, **bold**, lists).
                  Maths goes between dollar signs: $\Delta H$ or $$...$$.
                  "Insert a fill-in box" adds a small one-value box inside
                  a sentence.
  Numbered steps  Steps to follow; each step gets its own notes box.
  Data table      A table students fill in. Click a cell to make it blank
                  (students type there) or fixed. "Students can add rows"
                  lets them extend it.
  Question        A prompt with an answer box. "More options" adds a heading,
                  a hint, a required flag and a word-count target.
  Notes box       A collapsible box for the student's own notes.
  Photo slot      A place for students to add a photo of their work.
  Video           A YouTube link (unlisted videos work).
  Picture         A picture you upload. Choose Full, Medium or Small; small
                  and medium pictures placed one after another sit side by
                  side.
  Callout         A highlighted safety, warning, info, tip or hint box.
  Graph           A live chart drawn from one of your data tables; it fills
                  in as students type.
  Page break      Starts a new page when printed or downloaded.

Report tab blocks (the buttons under the Report list):
  Everything above, plus "Include a table", "Include a graph", "Include an
  answer" and "Include a photo", which copy a procedure block into the
  report so the student sees their own data there. Add anything to the Report
  list and students get a Report tab with a guided write-up.

Permanent names: every block that stores student work has a permanent name
(shown on the right of its card). The site assigns one when you first save.
Never change a permanent name on a lab students have started; their saved
answers hang off it.

Preview: the box under the Builder shows the lab exactly as students see it
and updates as you type. Click Refresh if it falls behind.

Importing: the Lab file view has "Import file", which loads a lab file
written outside the site. Claude can convert a PDF lab into this format using
the guide docs/LAB-FORMAT.md from the repository.


5. FOLDERS
----------
"+ Folder" or Edit on a folder card. A folder has a name, a link name, a
description, an optional cover photo with the same Zoom slider, an order
number, and "Who can open it". Folders can sit inside folders. Setting a
folder to "Selected student accounts" and ticking classes is the usual way
to give one course access to all its labs at once.


6. CLASSES AND ACCESS CODES
---------------------------
Students do not create accounts. You create a class, and the site makes one
access code per seat. Classes -> New class: give it a name, a short code
such as P3, and the number of seats.

Open a class to see its seats. Show reveals a seat's code; Copy list copies
all of them for a mail merge from your own email. Students click Log in ->
Access Code Login and type the code. It works on any number of devices, and
everything they type follows the code. The site stores no names or emails.
You can give a seat a nickname only you see, give it a new code if one is
shared or lost (the old one stops working at once), add seats, or close the
class at the end of term, which keeps all work but stops logins.


7. STUDENT WORK AND SAFETY
--------------------------
Everything a logged-in student types is saved to their account as they type,
and their photos are saved too, so a cleared browser or a new device gets it
all back on login. Visitors without a login keep work only in their browser.

Stats -> Students lists accounts; Classes lists seats. Either lets you open a
student's saved entries for any lab, read-only, with their photos, and export
a CSV. Do not delete seats or classes mid-term; close the class instead.


8. GET HELP
-----------
When Get help is on for a lab, a logged-in student can send a question from
the lab page. The ticked admins get an email within seconds, the Help button
in the admin bar shows a count, and the browser tab title shows it too.
Open Help, click the thread, type a reply. The student sees it on their lab
page immediately, or in their thread next time they open the lab. Mark
resolved when done; the student can still write back.


9. SITE TEXT, DESIGN AND THEMES
-------------------------------
Site text: every label, button and message students see, grouped by page.
Change wording there, never in the code.

Design: Palette (the site's default colours), Cards (size, image shape,
dividers) and Layout (page width, reading width, centring the lab column,
space above a lab, title size). Changes preview live; Save publishes them,
Cancel discards, Reset returns to the original look.

Theme boxes in the footer let each person pick Original, Paper or Graphite
(dark) for themselves. That choice follows a logged-in student between
devices and does not change the site for anyone else.


10. IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
----------------------------
Hard-refresh first (Cmd+Shift+R). Every change to the site goes out as a
new version and a cached old copy is the usual cause. If the site shows a
"saved copy" banner, the database could not be reached; wait a minute. Do
not edit labs while that banner is up.
